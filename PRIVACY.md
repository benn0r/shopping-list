# Privacy Policy

Shopping List does not collect, receive, retain, sell, or share user data with the developer. It has
no advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs, or developer-operated server.

**Effective date: August 23, 2026**

## About this policy

This policy describes the data practices of Shopping List, an iOS and Android app. Shopping List is
an independent app for scanning product barcodes and maintaining a local shopping list, with
optional synchronization to a Bring shopping list. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or
sponsored by Bring! Labs AG or the Open Facts projects.

## Data collected by the developer

None. The developer does not receive or store personal data, usage data, identifiers, diagnostics,
camera content, or shopping-list contents. Shopping List does not use advertising or analytics SDKs
and does not track users across apps or websites.

## Camera access

The app requests camera access only to recognize EAN and UPC barcodes. Camera frames are processed
on the device. Shopping List does not save photos or video and does not transmit camera frames to
the developer, Bring, or Open Facts services.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores only the information needed to work:

- your local shopping-list items, including barcode, label, brand when available, quantity when
  selected, and scan time;
- whether Bring synchronization is enabled;
- your Bring email address and password in platform secure storage when Bring synchronization is
  configured;
- your selected Bring shopping list;
- custom barcode labels; and
- product-lookup and language preferences.

This information remains in private app storage on your device and is not available to the
developer.

## Communication with Bring

Bring synchronization is disabled by default. When you enable it and sign in, retrieve shopping
lists, or add an item, the app communicates directly with Bring's services over HTTPS. Depending on
your action, it sends your Bring email address and password, user and session identifiers,
shopping-list identifier, item label, and an optional quantity. The scanned barcode is not sent to
Bring. Bring controls its own processing, retention, and deletion of this information.

## Product-information lookups

Unless you use a custom local label, the app sends the scanned barcode and a language and country
code derived from your device locale to the Open Facts product-information services. These include
Open Food Facts, Open Products Facts, Open Beauty Facts, and Open Pet Food Facts. Those services and
their hosting providers may also receive standard network information such as your IP address,
request time, and user-agent information. Their own privacy notices govern that processing.

## Sharing, sale, and tracking

The developer does not sell personal information, use it for advertising, create advertising
profiles, share it with data brokers, or track users. Information is sent only to Bring and Open
Facts when necessary to perform an action you request.

## Retention and deletion

The developer retains no user information. Logging out removes your Bring credentials and selected
Bring shopping list from the app. Custom labels can be deleted in Settings. Marking a local item as
purchased removes it from the local shopping list. Deleting the app removes its other local data,
subject to your operating system's storage and backup behavior. Information sent to Bring or Open
Facts is subject to those services' retention and deletion practices. Bring account requests must
be directed to Bring because the developer cannot access or delete that account data.

## Security

Bring credentials use platform secure storage. Other local records use the app's private
application storage, and network requests use HTTPS. No storage or transmission method can be
guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Children's privacy

Shopping List is a general-audience utility and is not directed specifically to children. The app
does not knowingly collect children's personal information on a developer-controlled server.

## International processing

Bring and Open Facts may process information outside your country. Their respective privacy notices
describe their processing locations and safeguards.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's functionality or data practices change. The effective date
at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer support contact listed on Shopping List's App Store or
Google Play product page.
