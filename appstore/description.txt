Shopping List - Scan & Shop keeps your shopping in one place: add items by typing or scanning a barcode, see them sorted by aisle, and shop together with your family.

YOUR CHOICE: ONLINE OR OFFLINE
• Offline: your lists stay on this iPhone. No account needed.
• Online: the free Shopping List Cloud with Sign in with Apple. Your email address is not requested.

SHARE WITH FAMILY
• Invite family and friends to your household with a link
• Everyone sees and edits the same lists, with changes appearing live
• Remove members or leave a household at any time

TELL YOUR AI
• Connect AI assistants through MCP and let them add, check off and read your lists
• "Add everything for lasagne" turns into items on your list

SCAN AND SORT
• Scan EAN and UPC barcodes; product names come from Open Food Facts and its sister databases
• Items are sorted into categories that follow the aisles
• Generated icons for every item in Cloud
• Custom labels for barcodes that aren't in any database

SHOP
• Choose a store and the lists you need, then tick items off as you go
• Undo a purchase and look back at past shopping trips

WORKS WITH WHAT YOU USE
• Sync individual lists with Bring!, Mealie or your own Shopping List Server

PRIVATE
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics
• Delete your Cloud account in Settings at any time; copies of your lists stay on your iPhone

Available in English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Dutch, Danish, Swedish, Norwegian, Finnish, Polish and Japanese.
