Shopping List - Scan & Shop on the Mac: plan your shopping at your desk, see items sorted by aisle, and take the same lists with you on your iPhone.

YOUR CHOICE: ONLINE OR OFFLINE
• Offline: your lists stay on this Mac. No account needed.
• Online: the free Shopping List Cloud with Sign in with Apple. Your email address is not requested.

MADE FOR THE MAC
• Items as tiles sorted by category, with an info pane for the details
• All your lists in the sidebar
• Keyboard shortcuts: ⌘N adds an item, ⌘R syncs, ⌘⌫ removes the selection
• Light and dark mode

SHARE WITH FAMILY
• Invite family and friends to your household with a link
• Everyone sees and edits the same lists, with changes appearing live
• Remove members or leave a household at any time

TELL YOUR AI
• Connect AI assistants through MCP and let them add, check off and read your lists
• "Add everything for lasagne" turns into items on your list

IPHONE AND MAC
• The same app on iPhone, with barcode scanning and a shopping mode for the store
• With Shopping List Cloud, your lists stay in sync on all your devices

WORKS WITH WHAT YOU USE
• Sync individual lists with Bring!, Mealie or your own Shopping List Server

PRIVATE
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics
• Delete your Cloud account in Settings at any time; copies of your lists stay on your Mac

Available in English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Dutch, Danish, Swedish, Norwegian, Finnish, Polish and Japanese.
